常見問題 · FAQ

MoriSnap 是訂閱制嗎？ · Is it a subscription?

不是。拍照和手帳功能免費、沒有廣告；VIP 是一次買斷，不會自動扣款。

No. Taking photos and keeping your journal is free with no ads. VIP is a one-time purchase and never renews.

換新手機後怎麼恢復 VIP？ · How do I restore VIP on a new device?

打開 MoriSnap → 設定 → 「恢復購買」，用同一個 Apple 帳號登入就好，不用再付錢。

Open MoriSnap → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. No extra charge.

共享手帳要付費嗎？朋友要裝 App 嗎？ · Are shared journals free?

共享手帳完全免費。朋友需要安裝 MoriSnap，並用自己的 Apple 帳號登入 iCloud。

Shared journals are free. Friends need MoriSnap installed and their own Apple Account signed in to iCloud.

不小心刪掉照片了？ · I deleted a photo by mistake

刪掉的照片會先放在「最近刪除」30 天，期間內都可以復原。

Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and you can restore them any time in that period.

照片會被上傳嗎？ · Are my photos uploaded?

不會。照片只存在你的裝置和你自己的 iCloud，我們沒有伺服器。詳見 隱私權政策。

No. Photos stay on your device and in your own iCloud; we have no servers. See the Privacy Policy.
